# Flat eyes without blur (2026-08-19)

Access date: 2026-08-19. Cursor-native research (WebSearch/WebFetch + 2 agents). No Grok Build.

## What “flat” actually is

Apple HIG / custom SF Symbols: **flat color fills, clearly defined edges, no drop shadows, no blurs, no feathered rims**. The system owns blur; the asset does not fake AA with `feGaussianBlur`.

Lucide: **no filters** in source. Figma SVG export is **1×**; blurs become rasters. Glass is not an SVG export.

## Why overlay + blur looked wrong

Two paints that share an outline each get their own AA, then `source-over` ([Compositing 1](https://www.w3.org/TR/compositing-1/)). Black-on-pink plus a clip plus a stroke plus a blur = fringe, then mush. `crispEdges` kills AA on the caps ([MDN shape-rendering](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/SVG/Reference/Attribute/shape-rendering)).

## Construction (Figma Subtract+Flatten / SVG donut)

1. **Under-plate:** two `--bg` stadiums, slightly larger (`EYE_PLATE = 0.8`).
2. **Candy:** one path, `fill-rule="evenodd"`: body + exact eye subpaths. Hole is unpainted, not a second fill ([SVG 2 paths](https://www.w3.org/TR/SVG2/paths.html); [MDN fill-rule](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/SVG/Reference/Attribute/fill-rule)).
3. **Clip** uses the same compound + `clip-rule="evenodd"` so chrome cannot enter the holes.
4. **No stroke, no eye filter.** Geometry stays the Figma/iOS smoothed capsule (ξ = 0.6).

The visible rim is **one** candy edge anti-aliased against the plate.
